# FOMO.ai Logo Assets

Official logo files. The mark is the "FOMO.ai" pill-in-pill wordmark.

| File | Use on | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `fomo-logo-on-dark.png` | Dark backgrounds | 500×500, transparent. White pill outline, white wordmark. Centered on a square canvas. |
| `fomo-logo-on-light.png` | Light backgrounds | 500×500, transparent. Solid black inner pill, black `.ai`. Centered on a square canvas. |
| `fomo-wordmark-light.png` | Dark backgrounds | 328×98, tight crop. The white wordmark used in the ad creatives. |
| `fomo-wordmark-dark.png` | Light backgrounds | 327×98, tight crop. The dark wordmark used in the ad creatives. |

All are transparent PNGs (RGBA).

- **Square `-logo-` files**: padded on a square canvas. Good for profile pictures,
  app icons, and anywhere a square mark is expected.
- **Tight `-wordmark-` files**: cropped to the mark. Good for headers, ad lockups,
  and inline placement. These are the files referenced by the ad creatives.

See `../../BRAND.md` section 3.3 for clear-space, sizing, and don'ts.
