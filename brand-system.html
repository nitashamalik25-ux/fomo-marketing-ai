04 Wordmark

Always FOMO.ai — caps, lowercase suffix.

Set FOMO in heavy display; mark .ai in Pulse. Keep the cap-height of "F" as clear space on every side.

FOMO.aiOn dark · white + Pulse suffix (preferred)

05 Voice & tone

A sharp, generous expert who wants you to win.

The voice is constant — these four pillars. The tone dial flexes by context: bold on a billboard, gentle in an error message.

Clear over clever

Short sentences, plain words. If a smart 12th-grader can't follow it, rewrite it.

Confident, not arrogant

Definitive statements, backed. No hedging, no boasting.

Urgent, never anxious

Name the stakes, then hand over the move. Pressure that empowers.

Proof over promises

Specifics, mechanisms, numbers — beat adjectives every time.
