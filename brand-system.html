04 Logo

The split-capsule wordmark.

FOMO.ai inside a rounded stadium pill, the signature shape. Echo the capsule in buttons and tags; never distort the mark itself.

FOMO.ai On white · split capsule (primary)
FOMO.ai On black · outline + white
FOMO.ai Brand-color fill · mono wordmark

Don't: stretch or rotate · add shadows/gradients · recolor individual letters · remove the pill · write "Fomo," "FOMO AI," or "Fomo.ai." It is always FOMO.ai.

05 Illustration

Hand-drawn doodles of people + AI.

FOMO's most distinctive asset: loose black-and-white monoline illustrations. Playful, human scenes of people working alongside friendly doodle robots. Color comes from the palette around the art, not inside it.

Style: loose hand-drawn line, bold black fills, white space. Quirky & warm.
Robots welcome, but only as charming doodles, never glossy 3D AI stock.
Color sits around the art: brand-tint backgrounds, pills, type. Optionally spot-color one element.
Always rounded: pills, circles, soft corners, echoing the logo.
06 Voice & tone

A sharp, generous expert who wants you to win.

The voice is constant: these four pillars. The tone dial flexes: bold on a billboard, gentle in an error message.

Clear over clever

Short sentences, plain words. If a smart 12th-grader can't follow it, rewrite it.

Confident, not arrogant

Definitive statements, backed. No hedging, no boasting.

Urgent, never anxious

Name the stakes, then hand over the move. Pressure that empowers.

Proof over promises

Specifics, mechanisms, numbers beat adjectives every time.
