01 · The Mission

Sell "leads without paid ads", using paid ads that actually convert

FOMO.ai gets brands found inside AI Search (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity) and traditional search, a managed AI + human content engine at a fraction of agency cost. This campaign drives cold and warm US audiences to a landing-page form for the 14-day free trial. Every asset is built to stop the scroll, frame the AI-search shift as urgent, and prove FOMO can be trusted with money-and-health-grade authority.

Primary KPI

Cost per qualified lead (CPL) → trial start. Secondary: landing-page CVR, hook rate (3-sec video views / thruplays), cost per landing-page view.

The funnel

Meta ad → message-matched landing page → short qualifying form → free-trial onboarding. No in-platform Instant Forms; we want intent + pixel data on-site.

02 · Who We're Talking To

Audience segments (US)

Three core segments. Each concept below is tagged for the segment it speaks to best. Run as separate ad sets so creative, copy, and CPL can be read cleanly per segment.

SMB founders & operators
Primary

10–200 employees. Founder, owner, head of growth. Wears many hats, no time for content, burned by agencies or thin AI tools.

Pain: "I know I'm invisible in search and AI, but I can't afford $8k/mo or a full content team."

YMYL brands
Health · Finance · Legal

Marketing leads at regulated, trust-sensitive brands (cf. Noom, PetMeds). E-E-A-T and authority are existential for ranking.

Pain: "AI only cites sources it trusts. In our category, generic AI content is a liability, we need credible authority at scale."

In-house marketing teams
Scale-ups

VP/Director of Marketing or Demand Gen at funded scale-ups. Owns pipeline, under pressure to cut CAC and reduce paid dependency.

Pain: "Paid is getting expensive and SEO is slow. I need an organic channel that compounds without hiring a team."

Targeting starters: Lookalikes from customer / trial lists, retargeting (site visitors, video viewers, IG engagers), interest stacks (HubSpot, Semrush, Ahrefs, marketing-agency interests, founder/SaaS communities), and job-title layering (Marketing / Growth / Founder) where available. Exclude existing customers.

✓ Do

  • One idea per asset; oversized headline reads in <2 seconds
  • Motion graphics, kinetic type, screen-capture & AI-generated/abstract visuals
  • Lead with the AI-search shift, the number, or the proof
  • High contrast; keep text out of Meta's 9:16 safe zones
  • Real customer names & logos (Noom, PetMeds, PB5star, HiveCast)

✕ Don't

  • No talking-head / UGC presenter video (production bottleneck)
  • No gradient mush, stocky handshake photos, or clip-art robots
  • Don't bury the hook below the fold or under a logo
  • No more than one accent color competing for attention
  • No unsubstantiated claims, keep YMYL copy defensible

Video without a face: all "video" concepts ship as AI-generated / motion-graphic assets, kinetic typography, animated UI mockups, chat-interface simulations, data viz. Captions baked in (sound-off default), ≤15s, hook in the first 1 second.

06 · Where The Click Lands

Landing-page alignment

Conversion is won after the click. Match the page to the ad that drove it, same hook, same promise, same visual. Build dedicated pages per stage (and ideally per top concept) rather than sending everyone to the homepage.

  • Message match: the ad headline reappears as the H1.
  • One CTA: start the 14-day free trial / book strategy call.
  • Short form: name, work email, website, company size, qualify, don't interrogate.
  • Proof above the fold: customer logos + a testimonial.
  • Risk reversal: "14-day free trial · month-to-month · cancel anytime."
  • Speed: fast load, sticky CTA, UTM-tagged per concept.
07 · Learn & Scale

Testing & measurement plan

Phase 1 · Angle test

Launch all 12 concepts at low budget across the 3 segments. Read 3-sec hook rate, CTR, and CPL. Kill the bottom third fast.

Phase 2 · Iterate

Take winning angles, produce 3–4 variants each (new hook, new format, new opener). Vary one element at a time.

Phase 3 · Scale

Consolidate winners into a scaling campaign; expand lookalikes; keep a continuous retargeting layer running BOF proof + offer.

Refresh cadence: introduce 2–4 new creatives every 2 weeks to fight fatigue. Naming: [Stage]_[Concept#]_[Format]_[Variant] for clean reporting.
