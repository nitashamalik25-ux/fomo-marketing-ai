# FOMO.ai Brand Design System & Voice

> **Purpose of this file.** This is the single source of truth for how FOMO.ai
> looks and sounds. It is written to be **portable across AI platforms**: paste
> it (in whole or in part) into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Canva, Jasper, or any
> LLM as context before generating copy, designs, ads, emails, or social posts.
> When you ask an AI to "write like FOMO.ai" or "design on-brand," give it this
> file first.

> **How to use it well.** For copy tasks, paste sections 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 8. For
> design tasks, paste sections 1, 3, 4. For a quick brief, paste the
> "One-paragraph brand brief" directly below.

> **Two house rules that override everything else:**
> 1. **Never use em dashes (—).** Use periods, colons, commas, or parentheses.
> 2. **One accent color per layout.** Blue leads. Don't mix the full palette.

**One-paragraph brand brief (the copy-paste starter):**
FOMO.ai helps businesses grow sales and leads **without paid ads** by combining
**advanced AI with seasoned human experts**. We specialize in **AI Search**:
SEO, AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), GEO (Generative Engine Optimization),
and LLMEO. These are the channels that now decide who gets found. Our promise is
the results of a premium agency at a fraction of the cost. Our voice is
**confident, plain-spoken, warm, and a little contrarian**. We are urgent about
the shift AI has caused, but calm and expert about what to do next. We sell an
**early-mover advantage**: the playbook that worked yesterday is breaking, and
the brands that adapt first win. Never fear-monger. Always hand the reader the
next move. (And never use em dashes.)

## 1. Brand Foundation

### What we do
FOMO.ai is an AI-plus-human marketing partner. We grow organic demand (sales
and leads that don't depend on paid advertising) by winning the new generation
of search: traditional SEO and the AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Google AI
Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini) where buyers increasingly start.

### The name is the thesis
"FOMO," fear of missing out, is reframed, not feared. AI has disrupted every
proven marketing tactic. Most brands are frozen. **The opportunity is to move
first.** We turn anxiety about being left behind into a concrete early-mover
advantage. The name should always read as *momentum*, never *panic*.

### Mission
Make world-class, AI-era growth accessible: premium-agency outcomes at a
fraction of the investment, for the businesses that can't afford to wait.

### Positioning statement
> For growth-minded businesses that can no longer rely on old marketing tactics,
> FOMO.ai is the AI-plus-human marketing partner that wins AI Search and
> delivers sales and leads without paid ads, combining advanced AI with seasoned
> experts to get premium results affordably.

### What makes us different
- **AI + Humans, not AI *or* humans.** Software for speed and scale, experts for
  judgment and quality. We never pretend one replaces the other.
- **AI Search specialists.** SEO, AEO, GEO, and LLMEO under one roof, built for
  how people search *now*, not five years ago.
- **No paid-ad dependency.** We build owned, compounding demand, not a meter you
  have to keep feeding.
- **Early-mover advantage, productized.** A clear "new growth playbook," not a
  vague retainer.
- **Premium results, fractional cost.** Accessible without feeling cheap.

### Brand personality (five traits)
1. **Confident.** We know this terrain and we state things plainly.
2. **Contrarian (constructively).** We'll tell you the old way is breaking.
3. **Pragmatic.** Every insight comes with a next move.
4. **Human.** Warm, playful, and jargon-free. AI *and* people.
5. **Energizing.** Momentum, optimism, "let's go." Never doom.

### Brand archetype
**The Guide** (with a streak of **The Pioneer**). The customer is the hero. We're
the expert who hands them the map to terrain we've already crossed.

## 2. Audience

### Primary persona: "The Stalled Operator"
Founder, marketing lead, or owner at an SMB or scaleup. Knows AI has changed the
game, watches old SEO and ad results decay, and feels behind. Time-poor,
budget-conscious, allergic to agency fluff. **Wants:** a clear plan and proof it
works. **Fears:** wasting money, falling further behind competitors who moved
first. **Speak to them with:** clarity, evidence, and an obvious first step.

### Secondary persona: "The Scaling Marketer"
In-house marketer at a growing company who needs to add AI Search capability
without hiring a full team. **Wants:** leverage and credibility. **Speak to them
with:** specificity about channels (AEO, GEO, LLMEO) and how we extend their
team.

### Audience rules
- Assume intelligence, not expertise. They're smart, but they may not know what
  "GEO" stands for. Define it once, then use it.
- Lead with their outcome (leads, sales, being found), not our mechanics.
- Respect their skepticism. Earn claims with specifics.

## 3. Visual Identity

> **Status:** Official brand colors, logo, and illustration style, codified from
> FOMO.ai's real assets. These hex values are the source of truth for every AI
> design task.

### 3.1 Color palette

FOMO.ai's identity is **blue and green** at its core (trust meets growth),
warmed by **gold** (premium value) and **coral** (energy, the FOMO spark). It is
a bright, optimistic, human palette, not a dark "tech" one.

**Primary: Blue & Teal (the brand leads with these)**

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Navy** | `#174D80` | Primary brand color. Headlines on light, depth, trust. The "serious" anchor. |
| **Blue** | `#438AE8` | Primary action color. CTAs, links, buttons, interactive, key highlights. |
| **Sky** | `#E9F2FE` | Soft blue surface. Section backgrounds, cards, callouts. |
| **Teal** | `#19866A` | Secondary brand color. Growth, results, the "AI Search works" signal. |
| **Mint** | `#93DBC8` | Supporting fresh accent. Tags, illustration fills, charts. |
| **Mint Tint** | `#DDF8F1` | Soft teal surface. |

**Secondary: Green / Gold / Coral families (accents & states)**

| Family | Hexes (deep · mid · tint) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | `#0C8253` · `#63C383` · `#C3EDD2` | Growth, success, positive metrics, "results" storytelling. |
| **Gold** | `#EBAA03` · `#FFE999` · `#FFF7DB` | Premium value, optimism, highlights, "early-mover" energy. |
| **Coral** | `#CA390B` · `#EB6553` · `#FFEDEB` | Urgency, the FOMO spark, warm CTAs, attention. Use sparingly. |

**Neutrals (from the logo & illustrations)**

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Black** | `#0E1116` | Logo, line illustrations, primary text on light. |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Primary ground. The brand lives mostly on white and light. |
| **Slate Grey** | `#5B6B7B` | Muted and secondary text, captions, hairlines. |

**Usage rules (read these before designing)**
- **One accent per layout. Blue leads.** `Blue #438AE8` is the default accent
  for almost everything: CTAs, links, eyebrows, highlights. Pick a *single*
  secondary color only when its meaning is needed (a green success metric, a
  coral urgency flag). Never scatter four colors across one screen.
- **The full palette appears in one place: documentation.** A swatch sheet or
  brand page may show every color. A real landing page, email, or ad should not.
- **Color = meaning.** Blue = action and trust. Green and Teal = growth and
  results. Gold = value and premium. Coral = urgency and attention. Stay
  consistent so the palette teaches the reader.
- **Light by default.** FOMO lives on white. Use soft tints (`Sky`, `Mint Tint`,
  `Gold #FFF7DB`, `Coral #FFEDEB`) sparingly, and rarely more than one tint per
  layout. Navy is for type and depth, not large fields.
- **Contrast.** Set body copy in Black or Navy, never in Blue, Gold, or Coral.
  Blue on white passes for large text and UI. Check small text.
- **Gradients:** keep subtle and within one family (Blue to Teal, or Sky to
  Mint). Never use a generic purple-to-blue "AI" gradient.

### 3.2 Typography

**Poppins is the brand typeface, used generally.** It is a geometric, rounded,
friendly sans (circular `O`s, soft terminals) that matches the wordmark. Use it
across headlines, body, labels, and data. Vary weight, not family.

| Role | Typeface | Weight | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / Headlines** | **Poppins** | **600–700** (SemiBold / Bold) | Friendly and confident. Slightly tight tracking on large sizes. `text-wrap: balance`. |
| **Body** | **Poppins** | **400–500** (Regular / Medium) | Line-height 1.5 to 1.65, roughly 65-character measure. |
| **Eyebrow / Label** | **Poppins** | **600**, **UPPERCASE**, letter-spacing `0.1em` | Small section tags. |
| **Data / Numbers** | **Poppins** with `tabular-nums` | 500–700 | Aligned figures for metrics. We sell measurable growth. |

**Fallback stack** (when Poppins isn't available, e.g. inside an email or a
locked-down tool): `Poppins, "Trebuchet MS", "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif`.

**Type scale (1.25 ratio):** 13 · 16 (base) · 20 · 25 · 31 · 39 · 49 · 61 px.

**Rules**
- One family (Poppins) everywhere. Create hierarchy with **weight and size**,
  not by switching typefaces.
- Headlines are rounded and friendly, never harsh or condensed. The geometry
  echoes the logo.
- Keep body weight at 400–500 for readability. Reserve 600–700 for headlines and
  labels.

### 3.3 Logo & wordmark usage

The logo is the **"FOMO.ai" wordmark inside a stadium (pill or capsule)
outline**: rounded, bold, friendly. The capsule is the signature shape. Echo it
elsewhere (buttons, tags) but never crowd or distort the mark itself.

**Construction**
- Wordmark reads **FOMO.ai**: `FOMO` in heavy rounded caps, `.ai` set tighter as
  the suffix. Lowercase `.ai`, always.
- Enclosed in a **stadium pill** with fully rounded ends.
- The pill is divided by a **curved (not straight) divider**, set off-center so
  the `FOMO` field is the larger left portion and `.ai` sits in the smaller
  right portion. The curve is the signature detail. Keep it, and keep it
  off-center.

**Approved lockups**
- **On white (primary):** the two-tone "split capsule." Larger left field filled
  Black holding white `FOMO`, smaller right field white holding black `.ai`,
  divided by the curve.
- **On black:** black ground, white pill outline, white wordmark, with the
  curved divider drawn as a white line.
- **Single-color:** all-black on light, all-white on dark, when print or
  contrast demands it.

**Rules**
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the `O` on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** legible at 24px pill height on screen.
- **Color:** brand colors may tint the pill *fill* (for example a Navy or Blue
  capsule) with a white wordmark, but the wordmark itself stays mono (black or
  white).
- **Don'ts:** don't stretch or rotate. Don't add shadows, outlines, or
  gradients. Don't recolor individual letters. Don't remove the pill. Don't place
  on a busy photo without a solid backing. Never write "Fomo," "FOMO AI," or
  "Fomo.ai." It is always **FOMO.ai**.

### 3.4 Illustration & graphic language

> This is FOMO's most distinctive visual asset. Lean on it.

- **Signature illustration style:** **hand-drawn, black-and-white monoline
  doodles.** Loose, organic strokes, bold black fills, playful and human scenes
  of people working *with* AI (chatbots, robots, devices, content, search).
  Quirky and warm, never corporate.
- **AI characters are on-brand** *in this style*: friendly doodle robots and
  chat bubbles. (This is the one place "robots" are welcome, because they're
  hand-drawn and charming, not glossy 3D stock.)
- **Color and illustration stay separate.** Illustrations remain black-and-white
  line art. Color comes from the brand palette *around* them (backgrounds, pills,
  type, accents). Optionally spot-color a single element in Blue for emphasis.
- **Shapes:** rounded everything. The stadium pill, rounded rectangles, circles.
  Echo the logo's geometry.
- **Layout:** bright, open, friendly. White space with the occasional soft tint
  block. Generous, never cramped.
- **Motion** (where relevant): playful but purposeful. A hover lift, a single
  reveal. Bouncy easing fits the rounded, human personality. Keep it light.
- **Iconography:** simple rounded line icons, consistent stroke, matching the
  doodle hand.

## 4. The "Don't look generic" guardrails (for design tasks)
When briefing an AI design tool with this system, explicitly forbid:
- Purple-to-blue gradient "AI startup" heroes.
- Glossy 3D robots, glowing brains, circuit boards, holographic blue UI (the
  photoreal AI-stock look). FOMO's robots are **hand-drawn black-and-white
  doodles** instead.
- Dark "premium tech" grounds. FOMO is **bright and optimistic on white**.
- Cold corporate stock photography of people in offices.
- Firing all brand colors at once. Use **one accent per layout, Blue leads**.
- Sharp, condensed, or harsh typography. The brand is **rounded and friendly**.
- Dropping the pill or capsule motif, or distorting the logo.

## 5. Voice & Tone

### Voice (constant)
FOMO.ai sounds like **a sharp, generous expert friend** who has already figured
out the new game and genuinely wants you to win it. Four pillars:

1. **Clear over clever.** Short sentences. Plain words. If a smart 12th-grader
   couldn't follow it, rewrite it.
2. **Confident, not arrogant.** We make definitive statements and back them. We
   don't hedge ("we think maybe...") and we don't boast.
3. **Urgent, never anxious.** We name the stakes (the playbook is changing) and
   immediately point to the move. Pressure that *empowers*.
4. **Proof over promises.** Specifics, mechanisms, and numbers beat adjectives.

### Tone (flexes by context)

| Context | Dial toward | Example feel |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage / ads | Bold, punchy, momentum | "AI broke the old playbook. Here's the new one." |
| Educational / blog | Patient, teacherly, clear | "AEO means optimizing to *be the answer*, not just to rank. Here's how." |
| Sales / proposals | Reassuring, specific, expert | "Here's exactly what the first 90 days look like." |
| Onboarding / product | Friendly, encouraging, brief | "You're set. First report lands Friday." |
| Error / problem | Calm, accountable, solution-first | "That didn't send. Here's how to fix it." |

The **voice** never changes. The **tone** dial moves. Bold on a billboard,
gentle in an error message.

## 7. Language System

### Words we use
early-mover advantage · the new growth playbook · AI Search · AEO / GEO / LLMEO ·
sales & leads · without paid ads · AI + Humans · compounding · be the answer ·
get found · move first · easy & affordable · seasoned experts · momentum

### Words we avoid
- **Hype / empty:** revolutionary, game-changer, cutting-edge, synergy, ninja,
  guru, supercharge, 10x (unless literally measured).
- **Fear-mongering:** "you're doomed," "falling behind forever," scare tactics
  with no exit. We name stakes *and* the move.
- **Robotic AI tropes:** "harness the power of AI," "AI-powered" as a crutch,
  "in today's fast-paced world."
- **Agency fluff:** "holistic," "bespoke solutions," "best-in-class,"
  "results-driven" (show results instead).

### Mechanics
- **Brand name:** always **FOMO.ai** (caps FOMO, lowercase .ai). Never "Fomo,"
  "FOMO AI," or "Fomo.ai."
- **Acronyms:** spell out on first use. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), GEO
  (Generative Engine Optimization), LLMEO (LLM Engine Optimization). Then use
  freely.
- **Capitalization:** sentence case for most headlines (feels modern, confident).
- **Punctuation:** **Never use em dashes (—).** Replace them with a period, a
  colon, a comma, or parentheses. Oxford comma yes. Exclamation points rare. Let
  the claim carry the energy.
- **Numbers:** use digits for stats (3x, 40%, $2k). Tabular when listed.
- **Person:** "you" for the reader, "we" for FOMO.ai. Active voice.

### Before / After rewrites (paste these to teach an AI the voice)
- ❌ "We leverage cutting-edge AI to supercharge your holistic marketing
  synergy." → ✅ "We use AI and expert humans to get you leads, without paid
  ads."
- ❌ "In today's fast-paced digital landscape, SEO is more important than ever."
  → ✅ "Search changed. AI answers questions now, and you can be the answer."
- ❌ "Our results-driven, best-in-class solutions deliver value." → ✅ "Premium
  results, a fraction of the cost. Here's the first 90 days."
- ❌ "Don't get left behind or you'll fail!" → ✅ "Early movers compound their
  lead. The sooner you start, the bigger the gap."
