**One-paragraph brand brief (the copy-paste starter):**
FOMO.ai helps businesses grow sales and leads **without paid ads** by combining
**advanced AI with seasoned human experts**. We specialize in **AI Search** —
SEO, AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), GEO (Generative Engine Optimization),
and LLMEO — the channels that now decide who gets found. Our promise: the
results of a premium agency at a fraction of the cost. Our voice is
**confident, plain-spoken, and a little contrarian** — urgent about the shift
AI has caused, but calm and expert about what to do next. We sell an
**early-mover advantage**: the playbook that worked yesterday is breaking, and
the brands that adapt first win. Never fear-monger; always hand the reader the
next move.

## 1. Brand Foundation

### What we do
FOMO.ai is an AI-plus-human marketing partner. We grow organic demand — sales
and leads that don't depend on paid advertising — by winning the new generation
of search: traditional SEO **and** the AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Google AI
Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini) where buyers increasingly start.

### The name is the thesis
"FOMO" — fear of missing out — is reframed, not feared. AI has disrupted every
proven marketing tactic. Most brands are frozen. **The opportunity is to move
first.** We turn anxiety about being left behind into a concrete early-mover
advantage. The name should always read as *momentum*, never *panic*.

### Mission
Make world-class, AI-era growth accessible — premium-agency outcomes at a
fraction of the investment, for the businesses that can't afford to wait.

### Positioning statement
> For growth-minded businesses that can no longer rely on old marketing tactics,
> FOMO.ai is the AI-plus-human marketing partner that wins AI Search and
> delivers sales and leads without paid ads — combining advanced AI with
> seasoned experts to get premium results affordably.

### What makes us different
- **AI + Humans, not AI *or* humans.** Software for speed and scale; experts for
  judgment and quality. We never pretend one replaces the other.
- **AI Search specialists.** SEO, AEO, GEO, LLMEO under one roof — built for how
  people search *now*, not five years ago.
- **No paid-ad dependency.** We build owned, compounding demand, not a meter you
  have to keep feeding.
- **Early-mover advantage, productized.** A clear "new growth playbook," not a
  vague retainer.
- **Premium results, fractional cost.** Accessible without feeling cheap.

### Brand personality (five traits)
1. **Confident** — we know this terrain; we state things plainly.
2. **Contrarian (constructively)** — we'll tell you the old way is breaking.
3. **Pragmatic** — every insight comes with a next move.
4. **Approachable** — expert without jargon-gatekeeping.
5. **Energizing** — momentum, optimism, "let's go" — never doom.

### Brand archetype
**The Guide** (with a streak of **The Pioneer**). The customer is the hero;
we're the expert who hands them the map to terrain we've already crossed.

## 2. Audience

### Primary persona — "The Stalled Operator"
Founder, marketing lead, or owner at an SMB or scaleup. Knows AI has changed
the game, watches old SEO/ad results decay, and feels behind. Time-poor,
budget-conscious, allergic to agency fluff. **Wants:** a clear plan and proof it
works. **Fears:** wasting money, falling further behind competitors who moved
first. **Speak to them with:** clarity, evidence, and an obvious first step.

### Secondary persona — "The Scaling Marketer"
In-house marketer at a growing company who needs to add AI Search capability
without hiring a full team. **Wants:** leverage and credibility. **Speak to
them with:** specificity about channels (AEO/GEO/LLMEO) and how we extend their
team.

### Audience rules
- Assume intelligence, not expertise. They're smart; they may not know what
  "GEO" stands for — define it once, then use it.
- Lead with their outcome (leads, sales, being found), not our mechanics.
- Respect their skepticism. Earn claims with specifics.

## 3. Visual Identity

> **Status:** Proposed system, grounded in the current fomo.ai look and ready to
> refine against finalized brand assets. Hex values are the source of truth for
> all AI design tasks until a brand-kit file supersedes this.

### 3.1 Color palette

**Core**

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#0C0F17` | Primary dark ground. Backgrounds, dark sections, footers. The default "FOMO is premium" surface. |
| **Slate** | `#1B2230` | Elevated surfaces on dark — cards, panels, dividers. |
| **Paper** | `#F5F6F8` | Primary light ground. Cool-biased off-white (never pure `#FFFFFF` for large fields). |
| **Mist** | `#9AA4B2` | Muted/secondary text, captions, borders. Cool grey biased toward Ink. |

**Accent**

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Pulse** | `#FF4D33` | THE brand accent. Urgency, momentum, CTAs, key highlights, the "FOMO" energy. Use boldly but in one place per view. |
| **Volt** | `#15CEB6` | Secondary accent — "AI Search / fresh / data." Used sparingly for contrast, charts, secondary tags. Never competes with Pulse. |

**Semantic (UI / data only — not brand accents)**

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Success | `#1FB979` | Positive states, growth-up. |
| Warning | `#F5B72E` | Caution, attention. |
| Critical | `#E5484D` | Errors only (distinct from Pulse — never use Pulse for errors). |

**Usage rules**
- **One bold per view.** Pulse is the star; let everything else stay quiet.
  Don't put Pulse and Volt at equal weight side by side.
- **Contrast.** Pulse `#FF4D33` on Ink passes for large text/UI; for body-size
  text on light grounds use Ink, not Pulse. Never set long copy in Pulse.
- **Neutrals are chosen, not default.** Use Paper/Mist, not raw white/grey.
- **Gradients:** allowed only as subtle Ink→Slate depth, or a faint Pulse glow.
  **Never** a purple-to-blue tech gradient — it's the generic AI look we avoid.

### 3.2 Typography

The personality comes from **weight contrast and scale**, not decorative fonts.

| Role | Stack | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Display / Headlines** | `"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, system-ui, sans-serif`, weight **800** | Tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`), large scale, `text-wrap: balance`. Confident, dense, grotesque. |
| **Body** | `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`, weight **400/500** | Comfortable line-height (1.5–1.65), ~65-char measure. |
| **Eyebrow / Label** | Same as body, weight **600**, **UPPERCASE**, letter-spacing `0.12em` | Small, used to tag sections. |
| **Data / Tokens** | `ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace` | Hex codes, metrics, code. Use `tabular-nums` for aligned figures. |

**Type scale (1.25 ratio):** 13 · 16 (base) · 20 · 25 · 31 · 39 · 49 · 61 px.

**Rules**
- Headlines are heavy and tight; body is calm and readable. The contrast *is*
  the identity.
- One display weight, one body weight per layout. Resist mixing many weights.
- Numbers/metrics in mono with tabular figures — we sell measurable growth, so
  data should look precise.

### 3.3 Logo & wordmark usage

> Final logo files to be added to `/assets`. Rules below apply regardless.

- **Wordmark:** "FOMO.ai" — set **FOMO** in heavy display, **.ai** in the same
  weight but Pulse or Mist to mark the suffix. Lowercase ".ai".
- **Clear space:** keep at least the cap-height of "F" of empty space on all
  sides.
- **Minimum size:** wordmark legible at 24px height on screen.
- **On dark (preferred):** white wordmark, Pulse ".ai".
- **On light:** Ink wordmark, Pulse ".ai".
- **Don'ts:** don't stretch, recolor the whole mark to Pulse, add shadows/
  outlines, place on busy imagery without a scrim, or rename to "Fomo AI" /
  "FOMO AI" — it's always **FOMO.ai**.

### 3.4 Layout & graphic language
- **Generous space, premium calm.** Whitespace signals "expensive done
  affordably." Don't crowd.
- **Grids and rules** over decorative blobs. Thin Mist hairlines, Slate cards.
- **Motion** (where relevant): purposeful and quick — a single page-load reveal
  or a hover lift. Momentum, never busy.
- **Imagery:** real dashboards, search results, and people — not generic robot/
  glowing-brain AI stock.
- **Iconography:** simple line icons, consistent stroke; Pulse only on the one
  icon you want noticed.

## 4. The "Don't look AI-generated" guardrails (for design tasks)
When briefing an AI design tool with this system, explicitly forbid:
- Purple→blue gradient heroes.
- Acid-green-on-black "tech" look.
- Glowing brains, circuit boards, humanoid robots, blue holographic UI.
- Everything centered; `rounded-lg` on everything; emoji as section markers.
- Warm cream + serif + terracotta editorial cliché.

Instead: Ink grounds, one Pulse accent, real product/results imagery, confident
heavy headlines, calm space.

## 5. Voice & Tone

### Voice (constant)
FOMO.ai sounds like **a sharp, generous expert friend** who has already figured
out the new game and genuinely wants you to win it. Four pillars:

1. **Clear over clever.** Short sentences. Plain words. If a smart 12th-grader
   couldn't follow it, rewrite it.
2. **Confident, not arrogant.** We make definitive statements and back them.
   We don't hedge ("we think maybe…") and we don't boast.
3. **Urgent, never anxious.** We name the stakes (the playbook is changing) and
   immediately point to the move. Pressure that *empowers*.
4. **Proof over promises.** Specifics, mechanisms, and numbers beat adjectives.

### Tone (flexes by context)

| Context | Dial toward | Example feel |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage / ads | Bold, punchy, momentum | "AI broke the old playbook. Here's the new one." |
| Educational / blog | Patient, teacherly, clear | "AEO means optimizing to *be the answer*, not just to rank. Here's how." |
| Sales / proposals | Reassuring, specific, expert | "Here's exactly what the first 90 days look like." |
| Onboarding / product | Friendly, encouraging, brief | "You're set. First report lands Friday." |
| Error / problem | Calm, accountable, solution-first | "That didn't send. Here's how to fix it." |

The **voice** never changes; the **tone** dial moves. Bold on a billboard, gentle in an error message.

## 7. Language System

### Words we use
early-mover advantage · the new growth playbook · AI Search · AEO / GEO / LLMEO
· sales & leads · without paid ads · AI + Humans · compounding · be the answer ·
get found · move first · affordable · seasoned experts · momentum

### Words we avoid
- **Hype/empty:** revolutionary, game-changer, cutting-edge, synergy, ninja,
  guru, unlock (over-used), supercharge, 10x (unless literally measured).
- **Fear-mongering:** "you're doomed," "falling behind forever," scare tactics
  with no exit. We name stakes *and* the move.
- **Robotic AI tropes:** "harness the power of AI," "AI-powered" as a crutch,
  "in today's fast-paced world."
- **Agency fluff:** "holistic," "bespoke solutions," "best-in-class,"
  "results-driven" (show results instead).

### Mechanics
- **Brand name:** always **FOMO.ai** (caps FOMO, lowercase .ai). Never "Fomo,"
  "FOMO AI," or "Fomo.ai."
- **Acronyms:** spell out on first use — AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), GEO
  (Generative Engine Optimization), LLMEO (LLM Engine Optimization) — then use
  freely.
- **Capitalization:** sentence case for most headlines (feels modern, confident).
- **Punctuation:** Oxford comma yes. Em dashes for momentum. Exclamation points
  rare — let the claim carry the energy.
- **Numbers:** use digits for stats (3x, 40%, $2k). Tabular when listed.
- **Person:** "you" for the reader, "we" for FOMO.ai. Active voice.

### Before / After rewrites (paste these to teach an AI the voice)
- ❌ "We leverage cutting-edge AI to supercharge your holistic marketing
  synergy." → ✅ "We use AI and expert humans to get you leads — without paid
  ads."
- ❌ "In today's fast-paced digital landscape, SEO is more important than ever."
  → ✅ "Search changed. AI answers questions now — and you can be the answer."
- ❌ "Our results-driven, best-in-class solutions deliver value." → ✅ "Premium
  results, a fraction of the cost. Here's the first 90 days."
- ❌ "Don't get left behind or you'll fail!" → ✅ "Early movers compound their
  lead. The sooner you start, the bigger the gap."
